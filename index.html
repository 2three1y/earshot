Talk to Earshot

Press Talk and speak. Press it again when you're done, or just stop talking. Try “read”, “pause”, “faster”, “what is this about?”, or ask a question about the text.

      Load voices

      These models run on your device. The first time, your browser downloads them from Hugging Face. After that they're saved in your browser and work offline. Nothing you read or say is uploaded.

      Settings

      Keyboard shortcuts

      Shortcuts work when you're not typing in a text box. Escape works everywhere.

      • K: Read or pause
      • S: Stop
      • Escape: Stop talking or listening
      • J and L: Back or forward one sentence
      • P and N: Previous or next paragraph
      • - and =: Slower or faster
      • T: Talk (start or stop listening)
      • A: Jump to the question box
      • W: Where am I?
      • ?: Jump to this list

      About Earshot

      Earshot is a mashup of open-source projects from Carter's GitHub stars. Neural speech is Kokoro 82M. Listening is OpenAI's Whisper or Distil-Whisper. Both run in your browser through Transformers.js and ONNX Runtime Web. Kokoro's pronunciation comes from eSpeak NG.

      Honest limits: the neural models are big for phones. On an iPhone, the first sentence can take a few seconds to start. Your device's voice and VoiceOver mode work instantly with no download. Web pages only load when the site allows it.
